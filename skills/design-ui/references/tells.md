# Tells

A tell is a choice that makes a page read as generated. Most tells began as good ideas on good sites and became tells by appearing everywhere without a reason. This list reflects late 2026, and new tells appear as models learn to avoid the old ones.

Replace a tell with a decision drawn from the subject, never with a named substitute font, color, or component. Every published "use this instead" list becomes the next wave of tells.

## Defaults

Avoid these unless the brief asks for them. The brief's own words always win.

Looks:

- A warm cream ground near `#F4F1EA` with a high-contrast serif display face and a terracotta or clay accent near `#D97757`.
- Near-black with a single acid-green or vermilion accent.
- A broadsheet layout with hairline rules, zero border radius, and dense newspaper columns.
- Purple-to-blue gradients, violet or indigo accents, and cyan on dark.
- The SaaS card kit: content chopped into identical rounded cards with one radius and the same soft gray shadow, plus gradient washes as decoration.

Structure:

- A centered headline with a subtitle, two buttons, and a screenshot below.
- The stock section order: hero, logos, three features, testimonial, pricing, and call to action.
- Rows of identical cards, each with an icon in a tinted square, a heading, and a line of text.
- The hero-metric template: a big number, a small label, supporting stats, and an accent.
- Section numbers such as 01 / 02 / 03 when the content is not a sequence.

Surface habits:

- One word in a headline set apart in italic, bold, or another color.
- Meta strings joined with middle dots (`A · B · C`), labels written as `WORD — fragment`, and `→` appended to links and buttons.
- Tiny letter-spaced all-caps labels.
- Monospace for labels that are not code, data, or measurements.
- Glass panels and backdrop blur as decoration, and floating pill navigation.
- Hard offset shadows such as `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0` outside a neobrutalist look.
- Tinted near-black such as `#0B0B0B` or `#111` standing in for black.
- The same fade-and-rise entrance on every section, and a hover transition on every card.
- Faces chosen by reflex: Inter, Roboto, Geist, Fraunces, Plus Jakarta Sans, and Space Grotesk.
- Sparklines, progress rings, and decorative shapes standing in for content.

## Bans

Never ship these. If the user asks for one, say that it is banned and why, and follow their answer.

- Gradient text. Use weight or size for emphasis.
- Emoji or Unicode glyphs used as icons.
- A colored `border-left` or `border-right` wider than 1px on cards, list items, callouts, or alerts.
- Cards nested inside cards.
- Content that stays hidden when a script or an animation fails to run.

`writing-for-humans` also bans eyebrow labels and invented proof.
